Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers Blockscape, a relaxing falling-block puzzle game published on Google Play by Naveh (Naveh Games), package com.naveh.Blockscape. It explains exactly what the game collects, what it does not, and why.

The short version: Blockscape never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. It shows no adverts, uses no advertising ID, has no sign-in, no chat and nothing to buy. It collects anonymous gameplay information and crash reports, used only to fix problems and make the game better.

What the game collects

WhatWhy
Anonymous gameplay events — which mode you play and at what speed, when a game starts and how it ends (score, lines and time played), lines cleared, rewinds used, the daily challenge started and finished, tutorial progress, whether you turned reminders on, and the settings you change. To find where the game is too hard, too easy or confusing, and to improve it.
Crash reports and diagnostics — when the game crashes or stops unexpectedly: the technical error, the screen you were on, and basic facts about the device such as its model, operating system version and available memory. To find and fix the crash.
Basic technical information collected automatically by Firebase — a randomly generated app instance identifier, the app version, device model, operating system version, language, and the country the device is in. To group the information above so that it can be understood.

The app instance identifier is a random number generated on the device. It is not linked to a person, and it is regenerated if the app is uninstalled and reinstalled or its data is cleared.

Your scores, best results, streaks, settings and saved game are stored on your device only.

Notifications

Blockscape can remind you that a new daily challenge is waiting or that your streak is about to end. Android asks your permission before the first one, and you can refuse, or switch reminders off later in the game's Settings, without affecting the game. Reminders are scheduled on your device: nothing is sent to us to make one happen, and we cannot send you a message.

Children

Blockscape is made for a general audience and treats every player the same regardless of age. It has no sign-in, no chat, no way for players to contact each other and no way to enter personal information. It shows no advertising and does not use an advertising ID.

What the game never collects

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • Precise location. (Firebase infers only the country.)
  • Contacts, calendars, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Payment information — the game sells nothing.
  • Your device's advertising ID.

Who else sees it

The gameplay information and crash reports are processed on our behalf by Google, through Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics). Google's handling of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy documentation. All of it is encrypted in transit.

We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long it is kept

Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days; both are then deleted automatically.

How to delete your data — step by step

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to close.

  1. Stop all collection. Uninstall the game. Nothing further is collected from that device from that moment on.
  2. Clear the data held on the device. Android Settings → Apps → Blockscape → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your saved progress and resets the random app instance identifier, so no future data can be connected to your earlier data.
  3. Ask us to delete the rest. Email navehohana@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, naming the game, your device model and the approximate dates you played. We will delete the anonymous analytics and crash records we can locate for that device, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept. Steps 1 and 2 delete everything held on your device. Step 3 covers what we hold: anonymous gameplay records and crash reports. We keep nothing that identifies you. Anything not deleted on request expires on its own (analytics after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days).

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's update description.
