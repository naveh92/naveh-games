Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers Nexusfall - Idle Tower Defense ("Nexusfall"), a game published on Google Play by Naveh (Naveh Games), package com.naveh.Nexusfall. It explains exactly what the game collects, what it does not, and why.

The short version: Nexusfall never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. It shows no adverts, uses no advertising ID, has no sign-in, no chat and nothing to buy. It collects anonymous gameplay and performance information, used only to fix problems and balance the game.

What the game collects

WhatWhy
Anonymous gameplay events — the waves reached and how each run ended, the upgrades and weapons picked, the diamonds earned and spent in the Armory, daily gifts, wheel spins, missions and Daily Rift results, the screens visited, the star rating you give if the game asks, and the settings you change. To find where the game is too hard, too easy or confusing, and to balance it.
Performance information — frame rate, the number of processor cores, approximate memory size, and similar technical facts about how the game runs on the device. To keep the game running smoothly on slower devices.
Basic technical information collected automatically by Google Analytics for Firebase — a randomly generated app instance identifier, the app version, device model, operating system version, language, and the country the device is in. To group the information above so that it can be understood.

The app instance identifier is a random number generated on the device. It is not linked to a person, and it is regenerated if the app is uninstalled and reinstalled or its data is cleared.

Feedback you choose to send

Nexusfall may ask you to rate it. The number of stars you tap is recorded as an anonymous gameplay event. If you choose to send feedback, the game opens your own email app addressed to navehohana@gmail.com; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and what you write is used only to reply to you and to improve the game.

Notifications

Nexusfall can remind you that a daily gift, a wheel spin or a new Daily Rift is waiting. Android asks your permission before the first one, and you can refuse, or switch reminders off later in the game's Settings, without affecting the game. Reminders are scheduled on your device: nothing is sent to us to make one happen, and we cannot send you a message.

Children

Nexusfall is made for a general audience and treats every player the same regardless of age. It has no sign-in, no chat, no way for players to contact each other and no way to enter personal information. It shows no advertising and does not use an advertising ID.

What the game never collects

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • Precise location. (Firebase infers only the country.)
  • Contacts, calendars, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Payment information — the game sells nothing.
  • Your device's advertising ID.

Who else sees it

The gameplay and performance information is processed on our behalf by Google, through Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase). Google's handling of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy documentation.

We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long it is kept

Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

How to delete your data — step by step

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to close.

  1. Stop all collection. Uninstall the game. Nothing further is collected from that device from that moment on.
  2. Clear the data held on the device. Android Settings → Apps → Nexusfall → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your saved progress and resets the random app instance identifier, so no future data can be connected to your earlier data.
  3. Ask us to delete the rest. Email navehohana@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, naming the game, your device model and the approximate dates you played. We will delete the anonymous analytics and performance records we can locate for that device, and any feedback email you sent, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept. Steps 1 and 2 delete everything held on your device. Step 3 covers what we hold: anonymous gameplay and performance records, plus any feedback email. We keep nothing that identifies you. Anything not deleted on request expires on its own after 14 months.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's update description.
