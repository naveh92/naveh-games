Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers Timmy and the Monsters, an action castle-defence game published on Google Play by Naveh (Naveh Games), package com.naveh.timmy. It explains exactly what the game collects, what it does not, and why.

The short version: Timmy and the Monsters never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. It shows no adverts, uses no advertising ID, has no sign-in, no chat and nothing to buy. It collects anonymous gameplay information, performance information and crash reports, used only to fix problems and balance the game.

What the game collects

WhatWhy
Anonymous gameplay events — which level was started and how it ended, how far you got, the weapons, talents and items you picked, fused or upgraded, the coins and diamonds earned and spent, tutorial progress, the menus opened and the settings you change. To find where the game is too hard, too easy or confusing, and to balance it.
Crash reports and diagnostics — when the game crashes or stops unexpectedly: the technical error, the screen you were on, and basic facts about the device such as its model, operating system version and available memory. To find and fix the crash.
Performance information — average frame rate, the number of processor cores, approximate memory size, and whether the game’s battery saver is on. To keep the game running smoothly on slower devices.
Basic technical information collected automatically by Firebase — a randomly generated app instance identifier, the app version, device model, operating system version, language, and the country the device is in. To group the information above so that it can be understood.

The app instance identifier is a random number generated on the device. It is not linked to a person, and it is regenerated if the app is uninstalled and reinstalled or its data is cleared.

Your progress, items, talents and settings are stored on your device only.

The game also downloads its balance settings (for example how tough a wave is) from Firebase Remote Config when it starts. That is a download to your device; nothing about you is sent with it beyond the basic technical information above.

Children

Timmy and the Monsters is made for a mixed audience that includes children, and it treats every player the same regardless of age. It has no sign-in, no chat, no way for players to contact each other and no way to enter personal information. It shows no advertising and does not use an advertising ID — the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app itself. We follow the Google Play Families Policy.

What the game never collects

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • Precise location. (Firebase infers only the country.)
  • Contacts, calendars, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Payment information — the game sells nothing.
  • Your device’s advertising ID.

Who else sees it

The gameplay information, performance information and crash reports are processed on our behalf by Google, through Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config). Google’s handling of it is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase’s privacy documentation. All of it is encrypted in transit.

We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long it is kept

Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days; both are then deleted automatically.

How to delete your data — step by step

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to close.

  1. Stop all collection. Uninstall the game. Nothing further is collected from that device from that moment on.
  2. Clear the data held on the device. Android Settings → Apps → Timmy and the Monsters → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your saved progress and resets the random app instance identifier, so no future data can be connected to your earlier data.
  3. Ask us to delete the rest. Email navehohana@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, naming the game, your device model and the approximate dates you played. We will delete the anonymous analytics, performance and crash records we can locate for that device, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept. Steps 1 and 2 delete everything held on your device. Step 3 covers what we hold: anonymous gameplay, performance and crash records. We keep nothing that identifies you. Anything not deleted on request expires on its own (analytics after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days).

Changes
